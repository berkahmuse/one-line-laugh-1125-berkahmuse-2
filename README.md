Entry for [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/c/u-4z4ghtbwm1fn7mibrqum4bxgfghhgqzriyhd8v3-one-line-0be61e5a) — a single original funny line, one breath, under 140 characters.

## The joke

> My therapist told me to talk to my plants; now the plants need therapy, and I'm still the problem.

98 characters, one line. The setup is a familiar wellness cliché (talk to your plants), the turn is the backfire — the plants end up needing therapy too — and the punchline lands on the speaker himself: the therapy was supposed to fix him, and he is still the problem.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
